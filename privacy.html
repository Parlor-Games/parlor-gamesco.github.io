Privacy Policy

Parlor Games Co. · Effective November 1, 2026 · Last updated November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information our mobile games collect and how it is used. It covers our apps on Google Play and the Apple App Store, including Sherlock Holmes Cryptograms (home-screen name "Holmes Ciphers"), published by Parlor Games Co. ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Our games are meant for adults and teens aged 13 and older. They are not directed to children under 13.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress, streaks, and settings (such as text size and high contrast) on your phone. We also create a random install identifier that is not linked to your name or contact details. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device.

Information we may collect through analytics

To learn which puzzles are too hard or too easy, and to improve the game, we may use an analytics service (for example, PostHog). When analytics is turned on, the app may send us anonymous events, such as which puzzle was started or finished, whether a hint was used, and which settings are chosen, together with the random install identifier and basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and app version. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

Advertising

Our games are free and are supported by advertising. When ads are shown, our advertising partners (for example, Google AdMob) may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalize them. Those partners have their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

You can limit ad personalization on Android by opening Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID. On iPhone, you can turn off tracking in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Some ad partners also offer their own opt-out controls.

How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To understand how the game is used and make it better.
  • To show and measure advertising.
  • To keep the game secure and fix errors.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the game, such as analytics and advertising providers described above, or when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping and deleting information

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Analytics information is kept only as long as needed to improve the game. Because the install identifier is not linked to your name, we may need it to find your data. If you would like us to delete analytics information associated with you, email us at the address below and include your install identifier if you can.

Children

Our games are intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to opt out of certain uses such as personalized advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive, such as using encrypted connections when data is sent from the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when we add a new analytics or advertising service, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
